Boundary corrections and a length-three extension of Bóna–Maga–Richey's extremal-frequency analysis

Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Published paper · Version 2 · CC BY 4.0

PDFSourceCitationRepositoryMathesis recordLean source

Abstract

For Bóna, Maga and Richey's extremal-frequency question (arXiv:2606.06655v2, revised September 9, 2026, Section 5.3, p. 19), we extend the classical frequency calculations of Lekkerkerker (1951), Chang (1986) and Kovačević (2018/2019) to classify the maximizers at lengths one, two and three by exact counting and generating functions. They are 0, 00, and the pair 100,001, respectively: the first two lengths contradict the unrestricted proposal, whereas length three agrees with it. We prove the authors' reducible-word frequency identity with its necessary boundary restriction, obtain all four complementary families, and show that their candidate frequencies decrease strictly to 1/2. The comparison with all competitors at lengths at least four is separate.
